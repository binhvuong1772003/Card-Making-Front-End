# Minimalism Nâu — source element notes

Source: <https://chungdoi.com/vi/mau-thiep/minimalism-nau/demo>  
Captured source: user-provided full `<body>` HTML, the separately pasted opening-card element and live DOM inspection in the existing Chrome tab.  
Scope: the opening cover, the invitation content page and the two popups (gift QR and RSVP). The cover is generated by `scripts/make-song-hy-covers.ts mbn`.

## Page structure from the source body

Root selector: `[data-testid="minimalism-brown-template"]`; `max-w-[480px] md:max-w-[900px]`, background `#fff7f3`, main text `#7c6a60`. The HTML is a long invitation page, with sections in this order:

1. Hero photo with the `frame-avatar` artwork and floating botanical decorations.
2. Wedding information panel, family details, names, date and ceremony details.
3. Album, 8 photos with a lightbox.
4. Reception details, calendar and RSVP.
5. Venue and map.
6. Dress code.
7. Five-row wedding schedule with `gate`, `cake` and `water` illustrations.
8. Guestbook form and visible guest wishes.
9. Gift section with one main gift, seven small gift images and a gift/QR dialog.
10. Thank-you footer.

Useful source selectors include `[data-testid="event-heading"]` and the sections carrying `data-landing-screenshot-id="invite-reception"`, `invite-rsvp`, `invite-map`, and `invite-guestbook`. Use these stable attributes before falling back to child-index XPath.

### Photographs and generated content

- Lead photo and album use 8 source photo-library URLs under `https://assets.chungdoi.com/photo-library/design/`; the first album photo is reused as the lead photo. The downloaded local files are `CMFE/public/demo/minimalism-nau/photo-1.webp` through `photo-8.webp`.
- All 17 decorative WebPs were retrieved from the rendered Chrome page and stored in `CMFE/public/demo/minimalism-nau/` and `CMBE/scripts/assets/decorations/`. The catalog seed created their MongoDB rows and uploaded the files to Cloudinary.
- The two `/api/qr-png?...` URLs are payment QR images, not decorations. Do not copy the source account numbers into the local template. Use a clearly nonfunctional local placeholder if a visual QR is needed.
- The source body includes music metadata. The local content-page plan keeps music off by default, consistent with the earlier request to disable music.

## Style and motion facts read from the HTML

- Main background: `#fff7f3`; primary text: `#7c6a60`; panel surface also uses `#f6eadd` and a light `rgba(255,250,245,.96)` layer. The gift heading is at y≈6559 and the main gift image at y≈6676.
- Main headings use Times New Roman; body uses Baskerville; large couple names use EB Garamond; date/body accents use Lora; handwriting uses The Nautigal; hero subtitle uses Cormorant Garamond.
- The source defines `@keyframes mbFloat`: translateY from `0` to `-10px` and back. Decoration instances use 5–6 second ease-in-out cycles with small per-element delays.
- The opening-card snippet separately identifies a 56px round heart seal and a 3-second shine sweep. It is deliberately excluded from this content-page task.

## Local CardMaking implementation

- `CMFE/lib/cards/demos/minimalism-nau.ts` generates the invitation from native `Text`, `Shape`, `PhotoFrame`, `Decoration`, `Album`, `DressCode`, `Calendar`, `Map`, `Form` and `Button` nodes. The start page is the cover (`public/demo/song-hy/cover-mbn.json`); pages are cover, invitation, `gift-details` (popup) and `rsvp` (popup).
- The 8 gallery photos are managed together in one editable Album block; the hero stays a separate PhotoFrame. The 7 gift minis remain separate Decoration nodes. The main gift stays as a static decoration on the page.
- Non-text layers receive readable names through `nameLayers`. They are not grouped because grouping drops the hand-tuned phone positions.
- Preview route: `/demo/minimalism-nau`. The reference template in MongoDB is seeded from the same `CardDocument`.
- Typography and section y positions use the desktop DOM measurements above. Photo collage placement and mobile positions are estimates; compare and refine them in the preview before claiming exact visual parity.

## Data boundaries

Record source-derived values separately from local layout measurements. Do not copy source bank details, QR payloads, tracking pixels, chat widgets, or brand footer link into the template. Upload reusable artwork to Cloudinary and create catalog rows in MongoDB; store album photos through the existing photo/image asset path, not as decoration entries. Every flower, leaf, frame, paper background, schedule icon and gift image should remain individually replaceable in CardMaking.

## Cover, gift group and popups (measured from the live source)

- Cover card 600x420 at the page centre: fill `rgba(255,250,245,.96)`, radius 8, shadow `0 25px 60px -12px rgba(0,0,0,.45)`. Page gradient `#f3e6da → #e8d5c4 → #dcc6b2`. Two `flower2-decoration.webp` images 137.8x320 (left rotated 25deg, right flipped and rotated -25deg, opacity .7) clipped by the card. Seal: 56px circle `#7c6a60`, white Material heart (28px), `seal-pulse` 2s. Names EB Garamond 36px `#7c6a60`, "&" 20px, ❦ rule, date Lora 18px at 72% opacity, "Thân Mời", pill button 140x48 `#7c6a60` with a 3s shine sweep. Twelve falling leaves `#c9a97e` (`ambient-fall`); opening plays `envelope-away`, `seal-break`, `seal-ring`, `dragon-fly-forward` and a 32-piece leaf burst in four browns.
- Gift group (button 260x280, left x=320, top y=6605 of the page): main gift 170x179, seven minis (centre/size/rotation read from the DOM, see `GIFT_MINIS`), shadow ellipse 144x12 (`igbShadowPulse`), four `✦` sparkles (`igbSparkleAnim` 2s, delays 0/0.6/1.2/1.7), hint "Nhấn để mở" 12px. Hover group "qua": main bounces, minis fly up, confetti. Click opens the gift popup.
- Gift popup: dialog 576x404, fill `#fff7f3`, radius 16, brown header 70px, backdrop black 40%; same column layout as Đỏ Đậm. QR images are illustrative placeholders, not the source payment QR codes.
- RSVP popup: 448px dialog with the "Xác nhận tham dự" form preset in brown.
- Still estimated: photo collage placement, phone placements of the content page.
